Title card → scene plan → image → 10s clip (+ extensions) → new scene at clip end.

How to use this

Plan scenes in 10 / 20 / 30 second blocks (one base clip + up to two extensions). Extensions stay in the same scene — you do not need last-frame language in extension prompts. When a clip chain ends, switch to the next scene. To stitch clips together in the final edit, grab a still of the last frame of a finished clip and use it as the starting image for the next scene’s video.
